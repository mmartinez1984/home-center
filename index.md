# Home Center

A personal home-control app for Android tablets.

Home Center is a private app built for one household. It shows and controls Sonos speakers, shows doorbell alerts, and reads and adjusts the owner's own Google Nest thermostat through Google's Smart Device Management API.

The app is not offered to the public and is used only by its owner on the owner's own devices.
