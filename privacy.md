# Home Center - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Home Center is a personal app used by its owner on the owner's own Android tablets. It is not offered to the public.

## Data the app accesses

With the owner's permission, the app uses Google's Smart Device Management API to read the owner's Google Nest thermostat (current temperature, humidity, mode and target temperatures) and to change the thermostat's mode and target temperatures.

## How data is used and stored

This data is used only to display and control the thermostat inside the app. A sign-in token is stored on the owner's own devices so the app can stay connected. The app does not collect, sell, share or transfer this data to anyone, and it has no servers of its own. Data is not used for advertising.

## Revoking access

The owner can revoke access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions or in the Google Home app.
